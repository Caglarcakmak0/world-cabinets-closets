Who We Are

Our Story

World Cabinets & Closets was founded by bringing together Ashley’s passion for design and refined aesthetic vision with Ethan’s engineering expertise. Together, we create thoughtfully designed living spaces that make homes more beautiful, functional, and comfortable.

We begin every project by developing a clear understanding of our clients’ needs, lifestyles, and personal preferences. From kitchen and bathroom remodeling to custom walk-in closets and personalized storage systems, we provide solutions that seamlessly balance aesthetics, functionality, and long-term value.

Our approach is built on high-quality materials, meticulous craftsmanship, transparent communication, and dependable service. From the initial consultation and design phase through production and final installation, we manage every stage with precision and treat each project with the same care we would give to our own home.

Client feedback is an essential part of our continued growth. It allows us to refine our processes, expand our selection of materials and finishes, and provide innovative design solutions that respond to the evolving needs of modern living.

At World Cabinets & Closets, we do more than renovate and organize spaces. We design distinctive interiors that enhance everyday living, improve functionality, and add lasting value to every home.
